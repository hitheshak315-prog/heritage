Curated temporary and travelling exhibitions shedding fresh scholarly light on Karnataka's art, craft guilds, and dynastic history.

Current Exhibitions

📅 September 1 – November 30, 2026
📍 Victoria Wing, First Floor Special Gallery

Gesso & Gold: Mysore Painting Traditions (1799–1920)

A comprehensive gathering of 45 restored sacred works executed using the distinct gesso embossing paste overlaid with genuine 24-karat gold foil. Discover depictions of Chamundeshwari, Rama Pattabhisheka, and courtly portraits sponsored by Maharaja Krishnaraja Wodeyar III.

Plan Visit to Exhibition
📅 August 15 – December 15, 2026
📍 Central Open-Air Epigraphy Court

Epigraphs in Granite: Deciphering Kadamba and Ganga Hero Stones

Hero stones (Veeragallu) and self-sacrifice memorials (Nishidhi) tell vivid stories of medieval bravery, border skirmishes, and companion loyalties. Featuring the famous 10th-century Atakur stone depicting a brave hound fighting a wild boar, accompanied by interactive estamperie rubbing displays.

Upcoming Exhibitions

📅 December 1, 2026 – February 28, 2027
📍 Diamond Jubilee Pavilion, Karnataka Heritage

Kingdom on the Tungabhadra: 500 Years of Vijayanagara Trade & Architecture

Commemorating the coronation of Emperor Krishnadevaraya, this major retrospective will unite architectural models, European traveler chronicles (Domingo Paes, Fernão Nunes), Persian diplomatic records, and gold coinage documenting Hampi’s international trade network.

Preview Event Schedule
📅 January 10 – March 30, 2027
📍 Armoury & Metallurgy Gallery

Crucibles of Fire: Wootz Steel and Weaponry of Peninsular India

A collaborative scientific exhibition with the Indian Institute of Science exploring the metallurgy of Karnataka's legendary ultra-high carbon wootz steel. Features 30 swords, armor suits, and iron rocket casings with microscopic metallurgical imagery.
