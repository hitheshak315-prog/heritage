Academic Division

Scholastic Resources & Fieldwork

Since the late 19th century under Benjamin Lewis Rice and Dr. M. H. Krishna, the research wing has published foundational surveys of South Indian epigraphy, numismatics, and temple architecture.

Today, our archives hold over 2,200 catalogued palm-leaf manuscripts, 1,400 copper-plate estamperies, and rare colonial survey monographs. We maintain a non-circulating reference library and a dedicated study room equipped with optical magnifiers and digital scanning terminals for accredited scholars.

📜 Digital Archives & Estamperies

Over 1,400 high-resolution monochrome ink estamperies of Kadamba, Chalukya, Hoysala, and Vijayanagara inscriptions documented across Karnataka's river valleys.

Format: 600 DPI TIFF & Open PDF

🪶 Historical Manuscripts Wing

Preserving classical works in Old Kannada (Halegannada), Sanskrit (Nandinagari script), and Prakrit on treated palm-leaves covering Jain philosophy, mathematics, and medicine.

Holdings: 2,200+ Catalogued Folios

🔬 Materials Conservation Lab

Publishes periodic technical reports on soapstone consolidating polymers, corrosion inhibitors for wootz steel, and bio-deterioration remediation in tropical museum climates.

Lab Facilities: XRD, FTIR & Microscopy

Scholar Services

Researcher & Student Access

We welcome PhD candidates, university faculty, independent historians, and postgraduate students:

  • Study Room Timings: Tuesday to Friday, 10:30 AM to 4:30 PM (Prior appointment required).
  • Handling Protocol: White cotton gloves and anti-static trays must be used when inspecting original folios or metal plates.
  • Photography Permissions: High-resolution RAW photographic captures for doctoral dissertations must be pre-approved by the Curatorial Board.
  • Digitized Microfilms: 12,000 folios available on on-site terminals with keyword indexing in Kannada and English.
