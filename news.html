Exhibition
📅 September 1, 2026 • Curatorial Press

New Special Exhibition: "Gesso & Gold" Opens to Public

The Directorate of Archaeology inaugurated the long-awaited retrospective on 19th-century Mysore school of painting. Gathering 45 rare masterpieces from royal collections, the display highlights traditional gesso relief and 24K gold foil embellishments.

Conservation
📅 August 18, 2026 • Preservation Lab

Restoration of 80 Hoysala Soapstone Brackets Successfully Completed

Our Material Conservation Laboratory has concluded an 18-month non-invasive chemical cleaning and desalinization effort on 80 chloritic schist bracket figures from Belur and Halebidu, stabilizing them against micro-flaking and airborne particulate wear.

Acquisition
📅 July 29, 2026 • Epigraphy Wing

Museum Acquires Rare 16th-Century Keladi Nayaka Copper Charter

A set of three engraved copper plates bound by a royal boar seal was donated to the museum by a Malenadu family trust. The inscription records an agro-irrigation grant issued by Keladi Venkatappa Nayaka in 1598 CE.

Education
📅 July 10, 2026 • Outreach Directorate

Annual Heritage Outreach Milestone: 120 Rural Karnataka Schools Engaged

The 'Museum on Wheels' educational bus visited high school students across Hassan, Mandya, and Chamarajanagar districts, bringing replica stone estamperies, interactive artifact kits, and archaeological documentary screenings to 14,000 children.

📅 June 24, 2026 • Public Programs

Registrations Open for National Halegannada Epigraphy Workshop

The museum invites history students and language enthusiasts for an intensive 3-day practical seminar deciphering ancient Kannada script variations from Kadamba, Ganga, and Chalukya hero stones. Certificates awarded upon completion.

Field Archaeology
📅 May 15, 2026 • Field Survey

Excavations Near Sannati Uncover Mauryan-Era Brick Stupa Foundations

Collaborative trial trenching along the Bhima river basin has revealed early Buddhist brick walls and inscribed potsherds in Mauryan Brahmi, confirming continuous monastic habitation between the 3rd century BCE and 2nd century CE.
