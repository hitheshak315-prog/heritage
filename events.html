Events & Cultural Programs

Engage directly with Karnataka's living traditions through guided heritage walks, master craft workshops, public lectures, and children's discovery programs.

15 Oct

Heritage Walk: Bengaluru’s Pete Relics & Kempe Gowda Boundaries

⏰ 07:30 AM – 10:00 AM 📍 Kempe Gowda Tower & City Market 🏷️ Category: Heritage Walk 🎟️ Fee: ₹250 (Includes Breakfast)

Trace the medieval urban geography planned by Hiriya Kempe Gowda in 1537 CE. Led by senior museum historians, covering water tanks (Keres), fortified bastions, and the bustling merchant quarters of Chikpete and Balepete.

Book Spot
22 Oct

Workshop: Hands-on Halegannada (Old Kannada) Script Reading

⏰ 11:00 AM – 03:30 PM 📍 Museum Epigraphy Lab, Ground Floor 🏷️ Category: Hands-on Workshop 🎟️ Fee: ₹300 (Kit Included)

A practical session on transcribing 5th to 12th-century stone inscriptions using paper estamperies. Participants learn the evolution of Kadamba, Ganga, and Chalukya letterforms under guidance from the Archaeological Survey epigraphists.

Register
01 Nov

Karnataka Rajyotsava Special: Yakshagana & Folk Drumming Gala

⏰ 05:00 PM – 08:30 PM 📍 Museum Open-Air Amphitheatre 🏷️ Category: Cultural Evening 🎟️ Admission: Free for Public

Celebrating Karnataka State Day with a full-length Badagutittu Yakshagana performance depicting the battle of Babruvahana, accompanied by thunderous Dollu Kunitha drummers and traditional bronze lamp illumination.

Free Entry Info
08 Nov

Memorial Lecture: The Hydraulic Marvels of Vijayanagara Hampi

⏰ 04:30 PM – 06:00 PM 📍 B. L. Rice Memorial Auditorium 🏷️ Category: Public Lecture 🎟️ Admission: Free (Open to All)

Delivered by Dr. Rameshwar Rao, Senior Hydrological Archaeologist. An illustrated talk investigating the network of stone aqueducts, siphons, and royal baths that sustained half a million inhabitants in medieval Hampi.

RSVP Seat
15 Nov

Live Demonstration: Channapatna Lacquer Craft & Woodturning

⏰ 11:30 AM – 04:00 PM 📍 Crafts Courtyard 🏷️ Category: Live Craft Demo 🎟️ Free Demonstration

Master craftspeople from Channapatna demonstrate traditional soft Wrightia tinctoria (Aale Mara) lathe-turning and non-toxic vegetable dye polishing methods introduced during the reign of Tipu Sultan.

Visitor Info
20 Nov

Junior Archaeologist: Simulated Excavation Trench for Kids

⏰ 10:30 AM – 01:00 PM 📍 Education Discovery Garden 🏷️ Category: Children (Ages 7–14) 🎟️ Fee: ₹150 (Includes Certificate)

Children dig in safe sand trenches to uncover replica pottery sherds, Roman coins, and terracotta figurines. Guided by museum educators, young explorers learn how to measure, tag, and sketch archaeological finds.

Enroll Child
26 Nov

School Heritage Day: Guided Dynasty Tour & Quiz Competition

⏰ 09:30 AM – 01:30 PM 📍 Main Museum Galleries 🏷️ Category: Registered School Groups 🎟️ Free for Government & Aided Schools

A curated interactive tour for middle and high school students with museum activity booklets, followed by a competitive Karnataka Heritage Quiz in the auditorium with book prizes.

School Booking

Program Participation Guidelines

  • All weekend heritage walks require advance online reservation due to group cap limits (max 30 walkers).
  • Workshops include all necessary art materials, archival paper sheets, and instructional booklets.
  • Government school visits are completely free of charge; school administrators should book 10 days in advance via our Education Wing.
