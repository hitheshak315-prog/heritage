Welcome to Karnataka Heritage

A Living Repository of Civilization

Established to preserve, document, and celebrate the exceptional cultural legacy of the land between the Godavari and Cauvery rivers.

From the rock-cut cave shrines of Badami to the colossal granite temples of Hampi, Karnataka's heritage is an unbroken dialogue of devotion, engineering, and fine arts.

The Karnataka Heritage Museum holds over 15,000 antiquities spanning the prehistoric, early historic, medieval, and colonial epochs. Our galleries present rare stone sculptures in chloritic schist, ancient gold and copper coinage, original palm-leaf manuscripts in Old Kannada, armaments from the Mysore Kingdom, and sacred bronzes.

As a public educational trust and cultural center, we welcome students, researchers, families, and international travelers to explore our permanent galleries, rotating exhibitions, and public lecture programs.

Current Special Exhibition

Gesso & Gold: Mysore Painting Traditions

On view in the Special Exhibition Wing through November 30, 2026.

📅 September 1 – November 30, 2026 | Daily 10:30 AM – 5:00 PM
📍 Victoria Wing, First Floor Gallery, Karnataka Heritage

Sacred Embellishments: The 19th-Century Court Painters of Mysore

Curated from the royal stores and private monastic collections, this exhibition gathers 45 rare original Mysore paintings created using the intricate gesso relief technique overlaid with 24-karat pure gold foil and natural vegetable pigments. Discover the distinctive serene iconography of Chamundeshwari, Rajarajeshwari, and the court life of Maharaja Krishnaraja Wodeyar III.

  • Includes live demonstrations by master craftspeople on alternate weekends
  • Free accompanying exhibition catalogue for school and college groups
  • Bilingual audio commentary available in Kannada and English
