Sculptures
350+ Catalogued Pieces • 6th–14th C.

Stone Sculptures & Sacred Bronzes

Exquisite chloritic schist Madanika figures from Belur, granite Dvarapala guardians from Hampi, 7th-century Aihole Nataraja bronzes, and Gommateshwara Jain stone icons.

Explore Artifacts →
Paintings
120+ Works • 16th–19th Century

Mysore & Vijayanagara Paintings

World-renowned Mysore traditional paintings featuring pure 24-karat gold foil gesso relief work, natural stone pigments, and copies of Lepakshi-style temple ceiling murals.

Explore Artifacts →
Coins
2,400+ Coins • 3rd BCE – 19th CE

Numismatic Treasures

Punch-marked silver coins of the Mauryas, Roman gold denarii unearthed at Chandravalli, Western Ganga gold elephant Gadyanas, and Vijayanagara Pagodas bearing Krishna Raya's seal.

Explore Artifacts →
Manuscripts
850+ Bundles • 9th–18th Century

Palm-Leaf & Copper Inscriptions

Extremely rare palm-leaf folios of classical Kannada works (Vaddaradhane, Pampa Bharata), Kadamba royal copper charter grants, and historical records preserved in Halegannada script.

Explore Artifacts →
Weapons
480+ Arms • 14th–19th Century

Royal Arms & Armoury

Crystalline wootz steel curved talwars, tiger-head hilts, Vijayanagara double-handed broadswords, chainmail coats, and iron rocket casings used in the Mysore-Anglo wars.

Explore Artifacts →
Pottery
620+ Vessels • Megalithic to Medieval

Terracotta & Ancient Pottery

Megalithic black-and-red ware vessels from Brahmagiri burials, burnished funerary urns from Maski, Roman rouletted pottery sherds, and decorative medieval temple roof tiles.

Explore Artifacts →
Jewellery
210+ Ornaments • 17th–20th Century

Temple & Royal Jewellery

Traditional Kempu ruby-studded head ornaments, Navaratna pendants, gold Kasina Sara necklaces, Nagara silver bridal belts, and gem-encrusted royal armlets (Vanki).

Explore Artifacts →
Traditional Objects
550+ Ethnological Artifacts

Folk Arts & Traditional Objects

Hand-carved wooden Yakshagana headdresses (Mudi), Channapatna vegetable-dyed lacquer toys, antique brass Gaja-Lakshmi hanging lamps, and handloom Ilkal weaver shuttles.
