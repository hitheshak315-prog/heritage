Key Historical Artifacts

Catalogued archaeological records featuring epoch, discovery origin, material composition, and curatorial analysis.

Epigraphy
c. 450 CE • Hassan District

Halmidi Inscription (Replica)

The foundational stone pillar bearing sixteen lines of the earliest known classical Kannada prose and Kadamba script, recording a battlefield military grant.

Accession: HK-EP-1894
Soapstone Sculpture
c. 1117 CE • Belur, Hassan

Shilabalika Bracket Figure

Carved from soft chloritic schist depicting a Darpana Sundari holding an ornate mirror. Shows micro-detailed bangles, tiered coiffure, and delicate bead necklaces.

Accession: HK-SC-1922
Royal Arms
c. 1795 CE • Srirangapatna

Steel Talwar of Tipu Sultan

Indigenous Karnataka wootz crucible steel blade with damascened watering pattern, gold inlays, and an intricately sculpted tiger-head pommel.

Accession: HK-WM-1901
Lost-Wax Bronze
c. 7th Century CE • Badami/Aihole

Badami Chalukya Nataraja

Sixteen-armed bronze of dancing Shiva enclosed in an oval Prabhamandala halo, cast using the cire-perdue method during the early Chalukya artistic experimentation.

Accession: HK-BR-1935
Numismatics
c. 1515 CE • Hampi Royal Mint

Gold Varaha of Krishnadevaraya

Struck during the zenith of the Vijayanagara Empire, depicting seated Lord Venkateshwara and goddess Lakshmi on obverse with royal Nagari legend on reverse.

Accession: HK-NU-1910
Palm-Leaf Text
c. 14th C. Copy • Shravanabelagola

Manuscript of Vaddaradhane

Oldest surviving prose text in classical Kannada, composed by Shivakotiacharya, inscribed with an iron stylus on treated talipot palm leaves.

Accession: HK-MS-1948
