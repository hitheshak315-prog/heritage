Room 1: Grand Museum Entrance & Foyer

Visitors enter through the iconic red brick neoclassical portico built in 1877 under Col. Sankey. The foyer houses a majestic life-size marble statue of Maharaja Chamarajendra Wodeyar X and an introductory relief map of Karnataka historical sites.

Curatorial Overview of Tour Rooms

🏛️ Room 1: Neoclassical Foyer

Preserves the 1877 foundation plaque, original Corinthian capitals, and life-size portraits of Mysore royal patrons. Serves as the starting point for guided tours and audio device rentals.

📜 Room 2: Epigraphy & Inscription Hall

Features 80 original and cast stone inscriptions. Major highlights include the 5th-century Halmidi Inscription and the 949 CE Atakur stone memorial recording a battlefield alliance.

🗿 Room 3: Hoysala & Chalukya Sculptures

Showcases 12th-century chloritic schist Madanika bracket figures from Belur and Halebidu, depicting celestial beauties, drummers, and musicians carved with lace-like precision.

⚔️ Room 4: Royal Arms & Numismatics

Houses over 400 wootz steel swords, tiger-pommel daggers, chainmail armor, and early Roman and Vijayanagara gold coinage reflecting Karnataka's military and trade history.

🎨 Room 5: Special Exhibition Wing

Currently hosting "Gesso & Gold: Mysore Painting Traditions," featuring 45 restored 19th-century works crafted with pure gold foil, natural vegetable dyes, and gesso relief paste.

Nothing compares to standing before thousand-year-old soapstone carvings in person. Plan your physical museum visit today.
