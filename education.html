Student Programs

Making History Tangible & Inspiring

History comes alive when children hold replica coins, trace stone inscriptions with their own fingers, and discover how ancient artisans carved enduring temples.

The Education Directorate of Karnataka Heritage partners with over 350 schools across Karnataka to complement social science textbooks with object-based learning. We offer structured gallery discovery worksheets, interactive workshops, and teacher training kits.

🎒

Curated School Tours

Guided tours tailored for Grades 5 through 12, focusing on the Karnataka State Board and CBSE history curricula covering Kadamba, Chalukya, Hoysala, and Vijayanagara dynasties.

Free Entry for Registered Schools
🏺

Junior Archaeologist Lab

A supervised simulated excavation trench where students uncover replica pottery shards, Roman coins, and terracotta beads while learning documentation and stratigraphy.

Includes Student Certificate
📜

Estamperie Rubbing Workshop

Students take home their own paper rubbings of the Halmidi Inscription and Kadamba characters made with authentic non-staining charcoal dabbers on wet handmade paper.

Hands-on Material Kit Provided

School Tour FAQs
