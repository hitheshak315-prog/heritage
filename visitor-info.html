How to Reach Us

Location, Transit & Parking

📍 Physical Address

Karnataka Heritage State Museum Complex
Kasturba Road, Opp. Venkatappa Art Gallery,
Adjacent to Cubbon Park, Bengaluru, Karnataka 560001

Landmark: 250 meters south of the General Post Office (GPO) and High Court of Karnataka.

🚇 Namma Metro Access

Cubbon Park Metro Station (Purple Line): Exit Gate 2. It is a scenic 600-meter stroll through the wooded park avenues.

Dr. B. R. Ambedkar Vidhana Soudha Station: Exit Gate 3 (800 meters).

🚗 Parking Facilities

Because the museum is located within the heritage Cubbon Park perimeter, dedicated museum parking is limited.

Authorized paid municipal parking is available at:
• Kanteerava Stadium Complex (500m)
• Queen's Road Multi-Level Parking (700m)

Inclusive Experience

Accessibility Services

Karnataka Heritage is committed to ensuring historical treasures are barrier-free and welcoming to all visitors:

  • Wheelchair Access: Ramp access is available at the main entrance portico and connecting corridors of the ground-floor galleries.
  • Free Wheelchair Loan: Available at the visitor reception desk on presenting valid ID.
  • Braille & Tactile Stations: Tactile resin models of the Halmidi Inscription and Hoysala bracket carvings are available in the Epigraphy gallery for visually impaired visitors.
  • Assistance Animals: Registered guide dogs are welcome in all public gallery spaces.
Gallery Etiquette

Museum Rules & Guidelines

  • Baggage Cloakroom: Backpacks, large umbrellas, and packages must be deposited at the free security cloakroom before entering.
  • Photography Rules: Handheld photography without flash is permitted with a valid camera pass. Selfie-sticks, tripods, and drones are strictly prohibited.
  • Preservation Care: Please do not touch stone carvings, inscribed slabs, or display cases. Natural skin oils cause irreversible chemical decay.
  • Food & Beverages: Consuming food, tea, or chewing gum inside galleries is strictly forbidden. Drinking water fountains are located in the central courtyard.
